# The Weekly Summary Post and Mentor/Teacher Weekly Summary windows roll from the day after posting, not a fixed Monday–Sunday week

Both digests used to compute their span from the _next Monday_ after the post — a fixed Monday–Sunday calendar week (two of them, back to back, for the Mentor/Teacher summary). That had a bug: "next Monday strictly after `postedAt`" jumps a full 7 days when `postedAt` is itself a Monday, instead of the 1–6 days it gives for every other weekday. A Mentor/Teacher Weekly Summary posted on Monday, Aug 17 2026 covered Aug 24–Sep 6 instead of the intended Aug 18–31 — an entire week silently skipped, discovered when a mentor noticed the posted window didn't match what should have been visible.

Both windows now start the calendar day after `postedAt` and run 7 days (Weekly Summary Post) or 14 (Mentor/Teacher Weekly Summary), whichever weekday the post lands on. `upcomingWeekRange`'s Monday-jump is gone entirely, not just patched for the Monday case, so there's no special case left to regress.

We considered fixing only the Mentor/Teacher summary, since that's where the bug actually surfaced, and leaving the Weekly Summary Post's Monday-alignment as is. We rejected that: both digests shared the exact same `nextMondayStrictlyAfter` function and the exact same failure mode, just less likely to trigger on the Weekly Summary Post's default Sunday-noon schedule — an admin changing `weekly_summary_time` to Monday would have silently hit the identical week-skip. Fixing one and not the other would also have left two digests describing "the week ahead" with different, inconsistent notions of what that means.

This changes what "This Week" means in the Weekly Summary Post: it's no longer a fixed Monday–Sunday calendar week, just whatever 7 days start tomorrow relative to when the post actually went out. ADR-0007's framing of the Mentor/Teacher summary's span as "two consecutive Monday–Sunday weeks" is superseded by this — the reasoning there (a 2-week buffer absorbs drift, so no mid-window edit-in-place is needed) still holds; only the calendar-alignment detail changes. `isWithinWeek` and Weekly Summary Change Reflection (ADR-0005) are unaffected — they compare against whatever range gets stored at post time, regardless of how that range was computed.
