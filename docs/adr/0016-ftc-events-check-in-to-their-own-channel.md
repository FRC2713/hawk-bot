# FTC-only meetings stay on the Team Meeting Calendar and check in to their own channel

Mentors who open the shop need one calendar that lists every time anyone is in the building, so FTC-only meetings stay on the Team Meeting Calendar. The cost, until now, was that each one got an Event Check-in Post in the announcements channel, where FRC-only students checked in to the wrong event and the channel filled with posts that weren't actionable for "all" of the team (issue #61).

An **FTC Event** is a Team Meeting Calendar Event whose title **starts with** a marker an admin sets (`ftc_event_marker`; the team's convention is 🟧), ignoring leading whitespace and case. It must lead the title, not merely appear in it: the team's joint meetings are titled like "Team Meeting 🟦 🟧 (FRC and FTC 26623 BtheBs)", carrying 🟧 as a tag, and those are for everyone and belong in the announcements channel. Only "🟧 FTC Team Meeting (36584 RAS)" is FTC-only. The convention to keep: a joint meeting never leads with 🟧, since one that did would post to the FTC channel only. It differs from every other Team Meeting Event in exactly one way: **its Check-in Post goes to `ftc_channel` instead of `announce_channel`.** Nothing else forks.

- **Hours are combined, not separate.** Attendance, the Reaction Cutoff, the Event Attendance Report and the Season totals all run unmodified. Season hours and attendance % sum over the events on a person's roster regardless of channel, so an FTC meeting counts toward the same totals as any other.
- **No new roster concept.** The Roster is already "the membership of the channel the Check-in Post went to, at the moment it posted" (`postCheckinPost` snapshots whatever channel it is given). For an FTC Event that is the FTC channel. Students not in the FTC channel are not on its Roster, so they are neither asked about it nor charged a No Response for it.
- **The Weekly Summary Post is unchanged.** FTC Events are still listed there, and the marker in the title is the tag. The digest has no reactions, so it can't cause a wrong check-in.
- **Calendar Role is untouched.** An FTC Event is `team_meeting`, synced, diffed, edited and removed like any other. Calendar Role says _which calendar_ an Event came from and is fixed at first sync; FTC-ness is a property of the title and can change with an edit.

Enabled only when **both** settings are set, the same shape as the Mentor/Teacher Summary needing its calendar and its channel. With either unset, every Event checks in to `announce_channel` exactly as before, so the feature can't half-work.

**Decided once, at post time.** The channel is chosen when the Check-in Post is sent and then lives in `events.checkin_channel`, which everything downstream already reads (reactions, resync, edits, lock, removal). Adding or removing the marker after the post is out does not move it; Calendar Change Handling reports the title edit as it would any other. Adding the marker before the post is out simply routes it.

## Alternatives considered

- **A fourth, FTC calendar as a Calendar Role.** The point of the issue is that mentors have _one_ place to look, and the team already invites the main calendar onto FTC events, so the events are already syncing. Reading a second calendar would add a cross-calendar duplicate problem, and a new `calendar_role` value means widening a `CHECK` constraint, which in SQLite is a rebuild of `events`, the table with the most foreign keys pointing at it.
- **A stored `is_ftc` column.** It goes stale when the marker setting is first set or later changed, needing a backfill for every event already synced. Deriving it from the title at post time needs no migration and has nothing to go stale.
- **Event colour, a hidden description tag, or guessing from "FTC" in the title.** Colour and description tags are invisible in the places people read titles (Slack digests, phone widgets). A text guess misfires on "FRC + FTC joint build". An emoji or bracketed tag in the title is already the team's habit, and visible everywhere.
- **Suppress FTC check-ins entirely.** Rejected by the team: FTC hours should land in the same log.

## Consequences

- **No migration, no new scope, no manifest change.** Two settings in `domain/settings.ts`; one pure function choosing the channel; `postDueCheckins` calls it.
- **FTC channel membership is now the FTC roster,** the same way announcements membership is the team roster (CONTEXT.md, Roster). A student who reacts but isn't in the channel isn't on the Roster and earns no hours. Keep the channel's membership current.
- **A student on both programs gets two posts,** one in each channel, which is the intended fix for the "checked in to the wrong event" confusion.
- **The No Response Alert ignores FTC Events.** Disengagement from the team is judged on Check-in Posts in the announcements channel only. Season hours and attendance % stay combined, but if a streak mixed the two, students on both programs would be judged on different events than FRC-only students under the same threshold, and FTC attendance could mask a student who has stopped answering the whole-team posts. `getRecentOutcomesForUser` therefore skips events whose `checkin_channel` is the configured `ftc_channel` (and filters nothing while the feature is off). It excludes the FTC channel rather than requiring `announce_channel`, so changing the announcements channel later doesn't erase everyone's history; if `ftc_channel` is changed, old FTC meetings can re-enter the window until it rolls past them.
- **Posting must not let one bad channel block the rest.** `postDueCheckins` currently has no per-event guard; an unset-up FTC channel (a private channel the bot wasn't invited to fails with `channel_not_found`) would throw on its first event and stop every Check-in behind it, FRC included, on every 5-minute tick. The loop catches per event, carries on, and rethrows an aggregate at the end so the scheduler's diagnostics still show the step as failed.
- **Slack may rewrite a typed emoji.** A slash command or message can deliver 🟧 as `:large_orange_square:`, which would store cleanly and never match a Google Calendar title. Validation rejects `:shortcode:`-shaped values and points at the web configuration page, which takes the real character; a plain-text marker such as `[FTC]` works too.

Worth revisiting if FTC wants its own Season figures or its own Attendance Report channel (both were left combined on purpose), or if more programs than FTC want the same treatment, at which point the marker and channel become a small list of pairs rather than two settings.
